NATIVE & HERITAGE TREE

Banyan

Ficus benghalensis

A magnificent evergreen tree recognised by its enormous crown, spreading branches and distinctive aerial roots.

A UNIQUE GROWTH FORM

🌳 Roots That Reach the Ground

One of the most remarkable features of the banyan is its aerial roots. These roots grow downward from branches and can eventually reach the soil.

01 Aerial roots

Roots develop from branches above the ground.

02 Support

Mature roots can become strong supporting structures.

03 Expansion

New supporting trunks allow the tree to spread across a large area.

BOTANICAL PROFILE

🌿 Banyan at a Glance

Family Moraceae
Genus Ficus
Growth Large evergreen tree
Leaves Broad and leathery
Fruit Small figs
Special feature Aerial roots

LIFE IN THE ECOSYSTEM

🐦 A Tree Full of Life

Banyan trees provide food and shelter for many organisms. Their fruits can attract birds and other animals, while their large crowns provide shade and habitat.

01 Food

Fruits provide food for wildlife.

02 Shelter

The large crown offers refuge and nesting spaces.

03 Shade

The broad canopy creates a cooler space beneath it.

BOTANICAL & CULTURAL NOTE

The banyan is one of India's most recognisable heritage trees, celebrated for its remarkable size, longevity and aerial-root system.
