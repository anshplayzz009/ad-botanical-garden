CONSERVATION

Protecting the Living World

Plant conservation is about protecting the diversity of plants and the ecosystems that depend on them.

WHY IT MATTERS

Plants are the foundation of life.

Plants provide food, oxygen, shelter and habitats for countless organisms. They also help maintain soil, water cycles and healthy ecosystems.

Conserving plants means protecting not only individual species, but also the relationships between plants, animals, microorganisms and their environment.

01
🌱

Plant Diversity

Protecting a wide variety of plant species helps preserve genetic diversity and strengthens ecosystems.

02
🐝

Pollinators

Bees, butterflies and other pollinators depend on flowering plants for food while helping plants reproduce.

03
🌳

Protecting Trees

Mature trees provide habitat, store carbon, reduce erosion and contribute to healthier landscapes.

04
💧

Water Conservation

Healthy vegetation helps protect soil and plays an important role in local water cycles.

05
🦋

Habitat Protection

Protecting plant habitats also protects the many insects, birds and other organisms that depend on them.

06
📚

Botanical Education

Learning about plants helps people understand why biodiversity deserves care and protection.

EVERYDAY ACTIONS

Small actions can support a healthier environment.

01

Grow suitable native or locally adapted plants where possible.

02

Avoid unnecessarily damaging wild plants and their habitats.

03

Provide suitable flowering plants and habitat for pollinators.

04

Learn the names and characteristics of the plants around you.
