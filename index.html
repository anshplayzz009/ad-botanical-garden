WELCOME

Explore the world of plants

AD Botanical Garden is a digital botanical journal created to explore and document the fascinating diversity of the plant kingdom.

Here you can discover information about different plant species, their scientific names, families, habitats, characteristics, uses and botanical significance.

🌿

A garden you explore digitally

AD Botanical Garden is entirely digital. It is an online educational garden and botanical journal, not a physical garden. There is no physical location to visit. Instead, explore plant information and botanical knowledge from wherever you are.
