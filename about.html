ABOUT THE GARDEN

Welcome to
AD Botanical Garden.

A digital space created to explore, document and appreciate the remarkable diversity of the plant world.

THE IDEA

A botanical garden without physical boundaries.

AD Botanical Garden is a digital botanical collection designed to bring the experience of discovering plants to the web.

Each plant can have its own field note containing information about its characteristics, habitat, ecological importance and cultural significance.

OUR PURPOSE

Observe. Learn. Protect.

01

Observe

Notice the plants around us and pay attention to their forms, adaptations and relationships with their environment.

02

Learn

Explore botanical information and develop a deeper understanding of plant diversity.

03

Protect

Understanding plants is an important first step toward appreciating and protecting the ecosystems they belong to.

04

THE COLLECTION

A growing botanical journal.

The collection begins with a small group of plants, but it is designed to grow over time.

New plants, photographs, observations, categories and botanical notes can be added as the garden develops.

CREATOR

Created by Ansuman Dakua

AD Botanical Garden is an independent digital project created by Ansuman Dakua as an educational and creative exploration of the plant world.

AD BOTANICAL GARDEN
