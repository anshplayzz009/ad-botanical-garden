Hibiscus

Hibiscus spp.

A striking flowering plant known for its large, colourful flowers and distinctive floral structure.

FLOWER SPOTLIGHT

🌺 The Hibiscus Flower

Hibiscus flowers are often large and colourful. Their prominent central reproductive column gives the flower its distinctive appearance.

Petals

Large petals create the colourful display that makes hibiscus flowers easy to recognise.

Staminal Column

03

Sepals

Sepals surround and protect the flower bud before it opens.
