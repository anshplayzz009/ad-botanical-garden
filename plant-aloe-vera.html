SUCCULENT PLANT

Aloe Vera

Aloe vera

A distinctive succulent recognised by its fleshy, pointed leaves and ability to store water in dry environments.

BUILT FOR DRY CONDITIONS

🌿 The Water-Storing Leaf

Aloe vera is a succulent. Its thick leaves store water, helping the plant survive periods of limited rainfall.

01

Fleshy Leaves

The leaves contain water-rich tissues that help the plant retain moisture.

02

Waxy Surface

The leaf surface helps reduce water loss in warm and dry conditions.

03

Leaf Margins

Small teeth occur along the edges of the leaves.

BOTANICAL PROFILE

🌱 Aloe Vera at a Glance

Family Asphodelaceae
Genus Aloe
Growth Succulent perennial
Leaves Fleshy and pointed
Flowers Tubular flowers
Habitat Warm, dry regions

PLANT STRUCTURE

🔎 Inside the Aloe Leaf

The leaf has several layers. The inner gel-like tissue is surrounded by other tissues that help protect and support the leaf.

01 Outer surface

Protective outer layer of the leaf.

02 Leaf tissue

Water-storing tissue inside the leaf.

03 Inner gel

Moist inner tissue found inside the leaf.

BOTANICAL NOTE

Aloe vera is a classic example of how plant structure can help a species cope with dry conditions.
